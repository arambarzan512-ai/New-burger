NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Choose dishes, add them to your cart, enter your contact and delivery details, then submit the order.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

This starter supports cash on delivery as a demo option. Real payment processing must be configured before launch.

How do I track my order?

Open “My orders” on this device to see saved demo orders and their latest status.

Can I add delivery instructions?

Yes. Add your street, building, floor or apartment, and any useful notes at checkout.
